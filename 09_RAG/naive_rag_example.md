# Aftab Company Employee Handbook

This fictional handbook is sample data for a RAG tutorial.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely for up to two days per week. Remote work requires approval from the employee's direct manager. Requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least one working day in advance. Employees must attend scheduled meetings and remain available during normal working hours.

## Annual Leave

All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal. Requests for more than three working days of leave must be submitted at least one week before the leave begins. The direct manager gives final approval. Submitting a request does not mean the leave has been approved.

## Working Hours

Normal working hours are 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Temporary changes require coordination with the direct manager. Employees must record their arrival and departure in the attendance system. Business travel requires a separate approved travel request.

## Laptop Requests

New employees must submit an Equipment for New Hire request through the IT service portal to receive a laptop. The direct manager must approve the request. After approval, the IT team delivers the laptop within two working days. The employee signs an equipment receipt that includes the laptop serial number.

## System Access

The IT team creates the corporate email account and HR portal access. Access to source code repositories must be requested by the team manager. Employees must not share passwords. Requests for additional access must include the system name and the business reason for access.

## First Week

On the first day, HR introduces the company structure and general policies. On the second day, the direct manager explains job responsibilities and goals for the first month. At the end of the first week, the employee attends a thirty-minute feedback meeting with the direct manager.

## Training Expenses

Job-related training expenses can be reimbursed after approval from the direct manager and submission of an invoice. The annual reimbursement limit is 500 dollars per employee. Approval must be obtained before registration. Registration without prior approval does not create a reimbursement obligation.
